Research

Ultrafast dynamics of amorphous ice and supercooled water

Using XFELs to follow structural change in amorphous ice after laser heating, reaching the deeply supercooled regime before crystallization. This line of work produced experimental evidence for a liquid–liquid critical point in bulk supercooled water, with the heat capacity diverging near 210 K.

Fast, immediate analysis during beamtime

Building the Python analysis that runs while the experiment is running, so decisions about where to take the measurement next are made on shift rather than months later. Several publications rest on data collected this way.

AI applied to X-ray scattering analysis

Bringing machine-learning methods to the analysis of X-ray scattering data — building on a background in both chemistry and computer science.
